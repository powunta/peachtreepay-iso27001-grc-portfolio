# Risk scoring methodology
All entries are **simulated inherent risks**. Analysts score likelihood (1 rare–5 almost certain) and impact (1 negligible–5 severe), multiply the two, and assign **Critical 20–25; High 12–19; Moderate 8–11; Low 1–7**.

For this case, impact includes patient-care disruption, ePHI confidentiality, system integrity, financial consequences and legal/regulatory considerations. Estimated residual scores are **hypothetical targets**, not validated results. No real risk is accepted without management approval and objective testing.

Steps: define ePHI assets and business owners → identify threats and vulnerabilities → assess existing assumed controls → score risks → select treatment/owner → identify necessary evidence → retest and reassess.
