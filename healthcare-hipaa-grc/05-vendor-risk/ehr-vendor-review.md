# VR-01 — Cloud EHR vendor assessment (fictional)
**Vendor:** CedarChart EHR, fictitious cloud EHR and support services vendor. The vendor is assumed to maintain ePHI for the clinic and may be a business associate.

| Review point | Simulated response | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Business associate agreement | Draft only | Block production ePHI transfer pending executed agreement if required |
| Privileged MFA | Vendor claims enabled | Request assurance evidence |
| Access logging | Monthly report promised | Review actual sample and retention terms |
| Encryption | Vendor self-attestation | Validate architecture and supporting controls |
| Incident response | Draft 72-hour contractual clause | Legal/privacy must assess; not a universal HIPAA notice deadline |
| Restore testing | Yearly test claimed | Obtain redacted test results |
| Subcontractors | Inventory missing | Obtain list and assurance |
| Remote support | Standing admin permissions | Require time-limited, approved sessions |

**Decision:** No production ePHI transfer until required legal and security conditions are fulfilled. Subsequent conditional approval requires accountable leadership and documented risk acceptance. The analyst does not unilaterally approve vendor access.

**Owners:** Privacy Officer, IT Lead, Legal. Links: R04 and R09.
