# Executive risk brief — Peachtree Health Clinic
**Date:** October 2026 | **Scenario only — no real patient or production data.**

**Purpose:** Summarize a sampled healthcare security assessment and seek management decisions.

**Scope:** Cloud EHR, patient portal, workforce identity, devices, backups, vendor support and ePHI exposure paths.

**Assessment:** 12 simulated risks: 3 Critical, 8 High and 1 Moderate. Top priorities are dormant EHR access, weak privileged authentication and ransomware-related care disruption. A synthetic offboarding test found 4 exceptions among 24 terminations; no actual unauthorized access was demonstrated. Vendor evidence and an applicable executed BAA require confirmation before proposed ePHI production processing.

**Management actions requested:** Approve accountable control owners; fund HR–IT offboarding automation; require privileged access controls; document backup-restore test; authorize legal/privacy vendor review; accept the 30/60/90-day plan.

**Success measures:** Reconciled departure/account records, zero unresolved overdue offboarding test exceptions, verified privileged authentication, evidence-backed restoration readiness, and vendor agreement/assurance documentation.

**Limitations:** No real system testing, audit assertion or HIPAA compliance conclusion. Proposed residual risk scores require evidence and management validation.
