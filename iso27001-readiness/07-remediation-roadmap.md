# 90-Day Remediation Roadmap

The roadmap sequences the most important readiness gaps by risk, dependency, and practical implementation effort.

> **Status note:** These are simulated planned actions. Completion should be supported by reviewed evidence and control testing.

## First 30 days

- Require company-wide MFA on critical systems.
- Formalize the HR-to-IT offboarding workflow.
- Define vulnerability-remediation timelines and escalation.

**Expected outcomes:** Reduced account-compromise exposure, repeatable termination controls, and accountable vulnerability handling.

## Days 31–60

- Perform an AWS security-configuration review.
- Implement quarterly access reviews.
- Centralize important security logging and alerting.
- Conduct an incident-response tabletop exercise.

**Expected outcomes:** Better cloud assurance, access governance, threat detection, and tested response roles.

## Days 61–90

- Formalize vendor risk tiering and security questionnaires.
- Strengthen recurring security-awareness and phishing exercises.
- Perform and document a backup-restoration test.

**Expected outcomes:** Risk-based supplier oversight, measurable human-risk controls, and recovery assurance.

## Ongoing activities

- Quarterly access reviews
- Vulnerability scanning and patch monitoring
- Vendor reassessment
- Security awareness
- Backup testing
- Risk-register updates
- Annual policy review
- Incident-response exercises

## Completion criteria

An action should move from **Planned** to **Complete** only when:

1. The control or process is implemented.
2. The accountable owner reviews the result.
3. Evidence is retained and linked to the requirement.
4. Testing confirms the control is designed and operating as intended.
5. The associated residual risk is reassessed and formally accepted or treated further.
