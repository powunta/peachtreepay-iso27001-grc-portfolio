# PeachtreePay GRC Portfolio - Executive Summary

> **Simulated portfolio work - not a real client engagement or certification audit.** PeachtreePay is a fictional 100-person fintech organization created for educational and job-portfolio purposes.

A [one-page PDF version](../PeachtreePay_GRC_Executive_Summary.pdf) is available for downloading and sharing.

## Purpose

This portfolio demonstrates how an entry-level GRC analyst can translate business context into risk decisions, control assessments, evidence expectations, remediation priorities, and management communication.

## Portfolio Scope

### Project 1 - ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Readiness

- Defined the ISMS scope and key assets
- Applied a 5 x 5 risk methodology
- Created and treated 15 information-security risks
- Assessed 12 readiness areas
- Documented a selected-control Statement of Applicability
- Mapped 19 evidence types
- Drafted policy samples and a 90-day remediation roadmap
- Demonstrated MFA traceability, cross-framework mapping, initial control testing, corrective-action validation, full-population retesting, and finding closure

### Project 2 - Third-Party Vendor Risk

- Assessed the simulated CloudBridge CRM vendor
- Reviewed questionnaire responses and evidence gaps
- Scored vendor risks and planned remediation
- Communicated a conditional-approval recommendation to management

## Key Findings

| Area | Executive result |
|---|---|
| Inherent-risk profile | 1 Critical, 13 High, and 1 Medium risk |
| Readiness status | 5 control areas Not Implemented and 7 Partial |
| Priority exposure | Inconsistent MFA creates the only Critical risk, scored 20 |
| Control assurance | Initial MFA testing failed because of two unsupported exceptions; corrective action and a 108-account retest later passed |
| Follow-up decision | MFA finding closed; residual risk 10 (Medium) supported with quarterly monitoring |
| Vendor decision | Conditional approval with tracked remediation and evidence requirements |

## Management Recommendation

Maintain quarterly MFA population and exception reviews following the simulated successful retest. Continue prioritizing formal HR-to-IT offboarding and vulnerability-remediation timelines, and require accountable owners, dated evidence, documented testing, and residual-risk review before closing the remaining remediation items.

## Business Value Demonstrated

The portfolio connects:

**Business risk -> inherent score -> treatment -> control alignment -> accountable owner -> evidence -> testing -> residual risk -> management decision**

This demonstrates risk-based judgment, audit-oriented evidence thinking, cross-framework awareness, and clear communication for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
