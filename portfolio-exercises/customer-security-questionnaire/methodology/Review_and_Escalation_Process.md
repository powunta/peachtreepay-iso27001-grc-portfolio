# Review and escalation methodology

**Simulation only:** No actual control owner approved these responses, and the evidence register lists hypothetical documents.

## Separate three concepts
1. **Control status:** `Met`, `Validate with SME`, or `Escalate` based only on the fictional scenario.
2. **Evidence verification:** Requires a real source, scope, currency, owner, and disclosure permissions; none were verified in this exercise.
3. **External approval:** Requires an authorized approver's explicit sign-off; **all responses remain unapproved**.

## Seven-stage workflow
| Stage | Owner | Required gate |
|---|---|---|
| Intake and scope | GRC | Confirm entity, product, and question context |
| Draft response | GRC | Avoid unsupported absolutes and promises |
| Verify evidence | GRC / control owner | Confirm source version, report coverage, and mapping |
| Validate with SME | Security, IT, Engineering, Product, Privacy, or Legal | Record reviewer and decision |
| Resolve exceptions | Appropriate risk/product owner | Approve transparent limitations, not invented commitments |
| Authorize release | Designated customer-assurance approver | Approve response and allowed attachments |
| Maintain library | GRC | Record owner, date, evidence version, and re-review schedule |

## Targeted review decisions
- **Q6:** Product / Security approve wording stating customer-managed keys are unavailable. Do not invent a roadmap date.
- **Q7:** Security Ops must validate staffed-versus-on-call after-hours alert coverage; do not claim 24x7 monitoring without confirmation.
- **Q10:** Legal should review contractual notification standards and separately applicable regulatory notification requirements.
- **Q13:** Privacy / Legal validates GDPR request handling, legal roles, exceptions, and deadlines.
- **Q17:** Legal / Security approves transparent wording on the incomplete AI risk assessment program.

## Evidence governance
EV numbers are traceability labels for hypothetical documents only; they are not links to actual evidence. A real process would check existence, authenticity, control period, sensitivity, permitted recipients, NDA, redactions, and the exact version disclosed.

## Lessons
Maintain a distinction between “the scenario states a control exists” and “real evidence supports sharing this claim externally.” Use repeated questionnaire gaps to inform documentation, risk, and product backlogs.
