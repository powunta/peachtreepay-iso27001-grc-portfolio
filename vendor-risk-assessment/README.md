# PeachtreePay Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment

> **Simulated portfolio project - not a real client engagement.** PeachtreePay and CloudBridge CRM are fictional organizations created for educational and job-portfolio purposes.

## Scenario

PeachtreePay is evaluating **CloudBridge CRM**, a SaaS platform that would store customer contact information, support-case notes, limited transaction-support metadata, and employee business-contact data.

The goal of this project is to demonstrate how a junior GRC or third-party risk analyst can evaluate a vendor before production onboarding, identify control gaps, score risk, recommend remediation, and communicate a business decision to management.

## Analyst Objective

Determine whether CloudBridge CRM should be:

- Approved
- Conditionally approved
- Rejected

based on the vendor's security posture, business criticality, data exposure, and outstanding risks.

## Risk Method

Risk score = **Likelihood x Impact**

| Score | Rating |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Low |
| 5-9 | Moderate |
| 10-16 | High |
| 17-25 | Critical |

This simulated assessment uses a 5 x 5 qualitative model consistent with the broader PeachtreePay portfolio.

## Final Decision

**Conditional Approval**

CloudBridge CRM may proceed only after the highest-priority control gaps are remediated or formally accepted by the appropriate PeachtreePay risk owner.

## Interview Walkthrough

> I performed a simulated third-party risk assessment for a SaaS vendor being considered by a fictional fintech company. I reviewed a vendor security questionnaire and supporting evidence, identified control gaps, scored vendor risks, created a remediation plan, and prepared an executive recommendation. I recommended conditional approval because the vendor had a generally mature security program but still had several material gaps that needed to be addressed before production onboarding.
